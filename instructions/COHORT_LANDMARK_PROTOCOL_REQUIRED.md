# Cohort and prediction-index checklist

The root `README.md` is the authoritative specification. Before examining
external outcomes, confirm and document:

- ADHD is identified using the supplied phenotype definition.
- ADHD index is the first qualifying ADHD diagnosis plus exactly 365 days.
- Non-ADHD patients have no qualifying ADHD diagnosis anywhere in their
  observable record.
- Non-ADHD index is selected from unique encounter dates of any type using the
  declared site seed, or an approved preassigned encounter-equivalent date.
- Recorded T2D on or before index is excluded.
- Observable follow-up continues beyond index.
- The primary cohort has no age restriction.
- No future T2D status is used for sampling, matching, or index selection.
- The primary external cohort retains its natural outcome frequency.
