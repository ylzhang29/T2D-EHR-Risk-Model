# ATC combination-product prerequisite

Confirm that the site can provide dated medication records using the supplied
ATC ingredient-level codes.

- Confirm whether combination products are already represented as separate
  ingredient-level records.
- If not, decompose them before applying the supplied lookup.
- If decomposition is not possible, document that combination-product exposure
  may be under-ascertained.

Medication records used in model development were prescription records; they
do not establish dispensing, adherence, continuous use, or treatment duration.

No additional ATC frequency audit is required unless the site is uncertain
about its medication mapping.
