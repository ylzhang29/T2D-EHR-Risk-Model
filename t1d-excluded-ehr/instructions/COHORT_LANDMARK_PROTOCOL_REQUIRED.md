# Cohort and prediction-index protocol

- ADHD index is 365 days after the first qualifying ADHD diagnosis.
- Non-ADHD patients have no qualifying ADHD diagnosis in observable history.
- Select the non-ADHD index from unique encounters using the declared site seed, or use a documented preassigned encounter-equivalent date.
- Do not use future T2D or post-index predictors for index selection.
- Apply no age restriction in the primary replication.
- Require observable follow-up after index.
- Exclude pre/index T2D, pre/index T1D, undated source-positive T2D, and at least two distinct on/before-index diabetic-range HbA1c/verified fasting-glucose dates.
- Retain gestational diabetes and the cohort's natural outcome frequency.
- Do not use medication evidence for exclusion or outcome ascertainment.

The outcome is the first recorded T2D diagnosis after index, not biological onset.
