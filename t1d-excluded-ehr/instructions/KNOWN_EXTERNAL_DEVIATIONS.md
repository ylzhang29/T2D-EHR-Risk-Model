# External deviation documentation

Document material differences in index construction, diabetes-free eligibility,
diagnosis definitions, laboratory units or fasting verification, medication
mapping, date precision, predictor construction, outcome ascertainment, death
capture, censoring, or subgroup availability.

Do not alter definitions after reviewing external performance. Primary results
must always report the frozen transported calibration. Any locally fitted
calibration is a separately labelled secondary analysis and does not replace
the primary result.
