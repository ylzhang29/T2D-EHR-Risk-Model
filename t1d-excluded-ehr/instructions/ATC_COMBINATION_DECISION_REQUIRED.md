# ATC ingredient prerequisite

Confirm that dated prescription records can be supplied using the exact
fifth-level ATC ingredient codes in the package.

Combination products must be decomposed into their ingredient-level records
before matching. Do not infer exposure from a broader ATC product class. If
decomposition is unavailable, document possible under-ascertainment.

Prescription records do not establish dispensing, adherence, continuous use,
or treatment duration. No additional ATC frequency audit is required unless
the site is uncertain about its mapping.
